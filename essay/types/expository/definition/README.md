# Definition Essay

> **At a glance.** Establishes what a term means beyond its dictionary line, especially for terms that are contested, abstract, or narrower/wider than common usage assumes.

A subtype of [Expository Essay](../README.md).

## Components

In the order they typically appear:

### Baseline Definition (required)

Gives the reader a shared starting point for the term before the essay extends or complicates it.

> "A 'third place,' as sociologist Ray Oldenburg defined it, is a space that is neither home nor work."

### Extended Definition (required)

Fills out the baseline definition with what the term looks like in practice, what it isn't, and what it resembles, so the reader can recognize it beyond the dictionary line.

> "A library is a third place; a self-checkout kiosk with no staff nearby to talk to is closer to a vending machine that happens to dispense books."

### Conclusion (optional)

Leaves the reader with why the essay mattered, not just a repeat of what it said.

**Note:** A weak conclusion restates the thesis verbatim; a working one synthesizes or states significance.

> "Faster checkout is a real gain — but a library that measures only speed will miss what it's trading away."

## Pitfalls

- A circular definition — the term defined using itself or a bare synonym with no added content.
- Extended definition that never says what the term is NOT, leaving its boundary unclear.
- Stopping at the baseline definition, which does no more work than a dictionary would.
