# Contrasts

A flat list of the points where the [Documents Consulted](documents-consulted.md) disagree, or
where one fills a gap another leaves. Each contrast gets a name for the question in dispute, and
says what each side claims, citing its documents by tag. Comparing the documents is what justifies
the decision — summarizing each in turn doesn't.

The [Decision and Rationale](../opening/decision-and-rationale.md) cites each contrast it settles,
by name and with the documents behind it, so a reviewer can trace every choice back to the sources
that forced it. A contrast the decision never cites is either not a real disagreement or a choice
the decision skipped — say which. Keep the list flat: a contrast is one question, and nesting them
hides which one a decision actually settles.

> - **Who queues at peak hours** — mostly quick one-item checkouts a kiosk handles [PITCH], against
>   a sizable group who would rather wait for staff [SURVEY].
> - **Running cost** — maintenance is left out [PITCH]; it's $1,200 a year per kiosk against
>   $38,000 for a second staffed register [COSTS].
>
> Decision: "Self-checkout kiosks, keeping one staffed register. *Who queues at peak hours*
> [PITCH][SURVEY] is why one register stays; *Running cost* [PITCH][COSTS] favors kiosks even with
> maintenance counted."
