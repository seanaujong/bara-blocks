# Summary

States the outcome up front or wraps up at the end — what changed, what the reader should now be
able to do, or what was decided — so a reader who stops here still gets the point. Often doubles
as the first thing a skimming reader sees (a PR description's title line, a design doc's TL;DR).

> "Patrons can now check out a book in under 15 seconds without waiting for a staffed register."
