# Before/After

A pair of screenshots (or a short clip) showing the same place in the product on either side of a
change, so the reader can see the difference instead of inferring it from prose. Only worth
including when the two states actually look different — a backend-only change has no "after"
screenshot worth taking.

> Before: the checkout screen shows a spinner for up to 90 seconds.
> After: the same screen shows a confirmed checkout in under 15 seconds.
