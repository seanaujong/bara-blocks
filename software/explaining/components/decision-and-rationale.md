# Decision and Rationale

States what was decided and why it wins on the tradeoffs that actually matter here — not a
restatement of the option's description. This is the component a future reader is actually
looking for when they ask "why did we build it this way?"

In a Design Doc the alternatives are hypothetical options weighed before anything is built; in a
Module Discovery the "alternatives" are the divergent readings of an existing module's boundary,
and the decision is which reading to commit to going forward.

> "Self-checkout kiosks: highest upfront cost, but the only option that removes the register
> bottleneck entirely and scales to more branches without adding staff."
