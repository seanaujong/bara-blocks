# Relationship

An arrow between two elements, labeled with what it does and — for Container diagrams and
below — the technology or protocol it uses. The label is what keeps a diagram from being just
boxes; "uses" alone says nothing.

Drawn at every zoom level, between whatever elements are in scope at that level: Person→System in
a Context diagram, Container→Container in a Container diagram, Component→Component (or
Component→Container) in a Component diagram.

> "Patron" —checks out books using→ "Self-Checkout Kiosk App"
> "Checkout API" —reads/writes→ "Catalog Database" [JDBC]
